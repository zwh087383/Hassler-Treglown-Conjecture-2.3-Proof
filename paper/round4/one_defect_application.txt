ALL SUM-FREE SEEDS WITH AT MOST ONE UNPAIRED ELEMENT
==================================================
This is a completed paper proof using the colored dominating-dissociation
lemma proved independently in round4/colored_dd_proof.txt. It includes
symmetric parts in all three external layers and arbitrary rank.

Colored lemma used.
Let R and Q be finite simple graphs on a common vertex set V. Suppose R
is triangle-free. Count U subset V such that U is independent in Q,
R[U] has maximum degree at most one, and U dominates R union Q. Then
there are at most 3^(|V|/2) such U. If R and Q share edges, move them to Q
only: the admissible U and union graph do not change.

Theorem. Let G=F_5 x H, h=|H|, n=5h, B={2,3} x H. Let S be sum-free,
S subset G\B, and suppose
    S = T union {s},    T=-T.
Then
    mis(L_S[B]) <= 3^(h/2) = 3^(n/10).
If s belongs to a nonzero external layer, the stronger bound
    mis(L_S[B]) <= 3^((h-1)/2)
holds. Consequently every external sum-free seed S with
    |S\(-S)| <= 1
satisfies the desired bound, by also invoking the already proved
symmetric case when S=-S.

Proof.
The element s is outside T, and symmetry of T implies -s is outside T.
In characteristic five, (1/2)s=3s=-2s. Expanding the loop set gives
    Lambda = B intersect ((T+T) union (s+T) union -(s+T)
                          union {2s,3s} union (1/2)T),
where the irrelevant point 0 from S-S lies outside B. Hence Lambda=-Lambda.
Every antipodal fiber
    C_x={(2,x),(3,-x)},  x in H,
is either entirely legal or entirely deleted. Let V subset H index the
legal fibers.

The T-generated graph has no edge within a legal fiber, since such an
edge would give 2z in T and hence a loop at z. Between any two distinct
legal fibers its edges are either complete or empty, because T=-T makes
z and -z have identical outside neighborhoods. Let Q be the simple graph
on V encoding the complete fiber-to-fiber edge sets.

Case 1: s=(0,a).
Here a is nonzero. Before loop deletion, put an oriented edge x -> x+a
on H. This is a disjoint union of directed five-cycles. Restrict it to V;
call the resulting underlying simple graph R. It is triangle-free, and
each vertex has at most one incoming and one outgoing edge. Writing
X_x=(2,x), Y_x=(3,-x), the s-edges over x -> x+a are exactly
    X_x X_{x+a},  Y_x Y_{x+a},  Y_x X_{x+a}.
The unique compatible pair across these two occupied fibers is
    X_x, Y_{x+a}.                                               (1)

For a maximal independent set I, define its support U by I intersects
C_x exactly when x belongs to U. Q-independence of U is forced by complete
fiber adjacency. If three consecutive R-fibers were occupied, (1) would
force the middle fiber to contain Y and X respectively, a contradiction.
Thus R[U] has maximum degree at most one. An isolated vertex of R[U]
corresponds to a fully selected two-vertex fiber, since it has no selected
Q- or R-neighbor to block the other vertex. Every edge of R[U] corresponds
to the uniquely specified pair (1). Thus U determines I uniquely.
An empty fiber with no occupied neighbor in R union Q would contain
undominated vertices, so U dominates R union Q.

For completeness, the converse also holds. Given such a U, take full
fibers at isolated vertices and the compatible pair (1) at each edge.
Independence is immediate. Each occupied fiber is dominated internally.
An empty fiber with an occupied Q-neighbor is completely dominated.
An occupied R-predecessor of an empty fiber is either a full fiber or
the right end of its occupied R-edge, in which case its chosen Y-vertex
is adjacent to both vertices of the empty fiber. Similarly an occupied
R-successor is either full or uses its X-vertex, again dominating both.
Thus the reconstructed set is maximal. This establishes an exact
bijection with the sets counted by the colored lemma, and gives
    mis(L_S[B]) <= 3^(|V|/2) <= 3^(h/2).

Case 2: s has first coordinate 1 or 4.
Negating if necessary and applying the shear (i,x) -> (i,x-i*b) for
s=(1,b), we may assume s=(1,0). These automorphisms preserve B and T=-T.
The fiber C_0 contains both forced loop vertices (2,0)=2s and (3,0)=3s,
so 0 is not in V and |V|<=h-1.

On each pair {x,-x} with x nonzero, the s-graph is the path
    (2,x) -- (3,x) -- (3,-x) -- (2,-x),
whose two bipartition classes are C_x and C_{-x}. It has exactly one
missing cross edge. Thus after legal-fiber deletion the s-graph is
represented by a matching R on V, each edge carrying a unique compatible
pair of singleton choices. A fiber isolated in the occupied support
must be chosen in full. An occupied edge of R uses its unique compatible
pair. An empty fiber with an occupied R-neighbor has a fully selected
neighbor fiber, because R is a matching, and is therefore fully dominated.
The same support argument consequently gives a bijection with the sets
counted by the colored lemma. Since R is triangle-free,
    mis(L_S[B]) <= 3^(|V|/2) <= 3^((h-1)/2).
This completes both cases. QED.

Overlap and deletions.
If a pair of fibers has both an R-edge and a Q-edge, the Q-edge makes
simultaneous occupation impossible. Deleting that R-edge therefore
changes neither R[U] for admissible U nor the domination graph R union Q.
Loop deletion only removes complete fibers; in Case 1 it changes five-
cycles into induced paths or isolated vertices, preserving all arguments.

This theorem does not imply the main F_5^k counting conjecture: seeds
with two or more unpaired elements under negation remain outside it.
