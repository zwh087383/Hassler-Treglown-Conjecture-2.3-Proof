MAIN THEOREM.
Let n=5^k and let f_max(F_5^k) count the inclusion-maximal sum-free subsets,
where sum-free forbids x+y=z even when entries repeat. Then, as k tends
to infinity,

                  f_max(F_5^k)=3^(n/10+o(n)).                (T)

The proof is assembled in Sections 1--7 below. The complete global
container/removal argument, large-seed lemma, and matching lower bound are
written in global_final_bridge_audit.txt.

1. THE GENERIC-BLOCK THEOREM, PRECISELY STATED
--------------------------------------------

THEOREM (GB). Let K be any finite-dimensional F_5-vector space of cardinality
kappa. Embed it in H=K direct-sum F_5 g, with g outside K. Let

  S subset {0,1,4} x K

be sum-free, and let Gamma_S be the link graph induced on

  {2,3} x ((g+K) union (-g+K)).

Then Gamma_S is loop-free and

                      mis(Gamma_S)<=3^kappa.                 (GB)

The graph depends only on the signed two-coset adjacency pattern, not on
the particular choice of g. The statement permits the seed tails to span
a proper subspace of K.

Write A=S_0, P=S_1, Q=-S_4, U=P union Q, and

  z=|A|, p=|P|, r=|Q|, a0=|A union -A|, d=|U|, s=z+p+r.

After exchanging the two nonzero layers, assume p>=r. In the coordinates
X_x=(2,x), Y_x=(3,-x), x in (g+K) union(-g+K), the edges are exactly

  X_x X_y: x-y in A union -A, or x+y in -Q;
  Y_x Y_y: x-y in A union -A, or x+y in -P;
  X_x Y_y: x-y in A,          or x+y in -U.

Difference edges preserve the residual sign and sum edges reverse it.
Since g and -g represent distinct nonzero cosets, the listed degree
contributions do not overlap. There are equally many vertices of degrees

             a=a0+z+d+p,       b=a0+z+d+r.                   (8)

In particular a>=b and a<=2b. When z>0, put alpha=a0+z>=3. Then

             a<=2b-alpha<=2b-3,     b>=a0+s>=s+2.            (9)

NORMALIZATION AND SUBSPACE CHECK.
The shear (i,x)->(i,x-it) preserves sum-freeness and the two B-layers.
All shears used below have t in the existing K. If the normalized seed
tails span L subset K, an old generic block splits into |K|/|L| generic
blocks for L. None becomes an exceptional core, because its original
nonzero residual coordinate is still outside K and therefore outside L.
An L-block bound 3^|L| therefore multiplies to 3^|K|. For the no-zero
matrix formulation, simultaneous translation of P,Q is just a relabeling
of the right columns; the same coset multiplication applies. Thus no
argument silently substitutes a lower-rank quotient for an arbitrary
covering graph.

Sections 2 and 3 prove (GB), according as z=0 or z>0. The empty seed gives
an edgeless graph and one MIS, so is covered separately without assumptions.

2. COMPLETE PROOF WHEN THE ZERO LAYER IS EMPTY
--------------------------------------------

With z=0, Gamma_S is bipartite with matrix

                   [ C_U   C_Q ]
                   [ C_P   C_U ],

where (C_T)_(x,y)=1 exactly when y-x belongs to T. Its two degree classes
are d+p and d+r. The full classification is also recorded in
matrix_nozero_remaining.txt, with its final status update.

2.1 Nested color sets, including all pure-layer seeds.

If Q subset P, the two left neighborhoods at each x are nested:

  N(L0_x)=(x+P) in R0 union (x+Q) in R1
     subset N(L1_x)=(x+P) in R0 union (x+P) in R1.

Selecting L1_x in an MIS forces selecting L0_x as well, since otherwise
L0_x could be added. Each left pair has only three possible states:
empty, {L0_x}, or both. A bipartite MIS is uniquely determined by its
left part, since its right part is the complement of the left neighborhood.
Hence mis<=3^kappa. The other inclusion reverses the two roles.
This proof includes P or Q empty, P=Q, and the empty seed. It reproduces
the non-enumerative nested-row argument without importing the finite
enumerations elsewhere in the historical round-two file.

2.2 Every affine-rank-one mixed case: the 212 bound.

When P,Q are nonempty and their union is contained in an affine line,
translate the union into a one-dimensional subspace. The matrix decomposes
into twenty-vertex graphs, one per five-element coset. Each such graph
has a perfect C4 factor: for fixed p0 in P, q0 in Q the four vertices

                  L0_x,L1_x,R0_(x+p0),R1_(x+q0)

span K_(2,2), and these five blocks partition all vertices. If a,b count
occupied left and right pairs in an MIS, then a+b<=5. Those with a<=2
are determined by at most 1+5*3+10*9=106 left patterns. Those with a>=3
have b<=2 and are determined by at most 106 right patterns. Thus every
component has at most 212<3^5 MIS, proving the target after multiplication.
This is a direct counting proof, with no rank-one enumeration. The same
argument appears in matrix_typeB_entropy_proof.txt, Section 7.

2.3 Seed size at most three.

Nested cases are settled by 2.1. Two distinct nonnested singleton color
sets normalize to affine rank one and use 2.2. The only remaining
three-seed case has a disjoint 2+1 color split. Translate the singleton
to zero. Its other two translations either have rank one (2.2), or are
independent and become P={0}, Q={e1,e2}, up to interchanging colors.

The latter is proved by matrix_rank2_transfer_proof.txt. The 83 carry
states have a direct cyclic-word characterization. Its 19-row rational

and Cauchy--Schwarz gives

                  mis <=(1433053/32)^(5/2)<3^25.

The explicit transition formula and integer table form the certificate.
The old round-two BDD is unnecessary.

2.4 Seed size at least seven.

Use global_trianglefree_potential.txt. The graph is bipartite and hence
triangle-free. Its three corollaries give the target in these regimes:

  max(a,b)<=10 and a+b>=17;
  max(a,b)<=12 and a+b>=18;
  max(a,b)>=12 and min(a,b)>=max(a,b)/2.

To see exhaustiveness directly, if p+r>=8 and p>=6, then a>=12; if p=5,
then r>=3 and the degree sum is at least18; the sole remaining possibility

unordered positive splits of seven.

4. WHY PREVIOUS FULL-LINK THEOREMS APPLY TO GENERIC BLOCKS
--------------------------------------------------------

The symmetric-background theorems used above are:

  round3/paper_algebra_lowrank.txt, Section8: symmetric seeds;
  round4/one_defect_application.txt: symmetric background plus at most
      one unpaired seed;
  round4/two_opposite_defects_application.txt: symmetric background
      plus two unpaired seeds in opposite nonzero layers;
  round4/colored_dd_proof.txt: their colored dominating-dissociation
      counting input.

If one uses a full-link version of any such theorem, the following
argument justifies the generic conclusion without ignoring a core.
Keep the seed fixed over K and extend H=K direct-sum F_5^m. If its
generic-block MIS count is C, the generic block occurs(5^m-1)/2 times,
and the core has at least one MIS. A full-link bound3^(|H|/2) gives

                 C^((5^m-1)/2)<=3^(kappa 5^m/2).

Let m tend to infinity. It follows that C<=3^kappa. Stronger full-link
bounds only improve this conclusion. The seed remains sum-free in
every extension. This is an explicit amplification argument; it is not
an assumption that a generic-block count equals a full-link count.

6. UNIFORM LOCAL LINK BOUND
--------------------------

The self-contained fingerprint lemma in global_final_bridge_audit.txt
shows that every simple graph with delta>=4096 and Delta<=2delta has

        log_3 mis/N <1/4-61409/24166404.

For a full standard-block link, retaining all vertices and ignoring
loops gives delta>=|S|/2 and Delta<=2delta. The original looped MIS count
is no larger: its legal-vertex induced graph embeds injectively by
maximal-independent-set extension into the ordinary graph. Thus
|S|>=8192 gives the target3^(n/10) directly.

For |S|<=8191, the seed-tail span K has kappa<=5^8191. The full link
consists of one core on2kappa vertices and(h/kappa-1)/2 generic blocks,
where h=n/5. Apply Moon--Moser to the core and (GB) to each generic block:

  mis(L_S[B])<=3^(2kappa/3)(3^kappa)^((h/kappa-1)/2)
              =3^(n/10+kappa/6)
              <=3^(n/10+C),   C=5^8191/6.                  (10)

Together the two ranges prove (10) for every sum-free seed outside B,
with the same absolute constant C. It is consequently uniform over
all seeds supplied by all containers.

7. COMPLETION OF THE GLOBAL PROOF
--------------------------------

Green--Ruzsa containers and Green's arithmetic removal theorem produce
2^o(n) pairs(M,E), covering every sum-free set, with M globally maximal
sum-free, E disjoint from M, and |E|=o(n) uniformly. For a globally
maximal sum-free X contained in M union E, put S=X intersect E and
I=X intersect M. Then I is maximal independent in L_S[M]. For each
pair there are at most2^|E| seed choices, and for each seed the map
X->I is injective.

If |M|<=3n/10, Moon--Moser bounds the link by3^(n/10). If |M|>3n/10,
Lev's published Theorem3 puts M inside two opposite cosets of a
hyperplane. Since M is globally maximal and their union is sum-free,
M equals that union. A linear automorphism sends it to the standard
block B={2,3}xF_5^(k-1), and (10) applies. Summing gives

                    f_max(F_5^k)<=3^(n/10+o(n)).

For the lower bound take S={(1,0)}. Its link on B has two forbidden
core vertices and(h-1)/2 disjoint four-vertex paths. Therefore it has
3^((h-1)/2) maximal independent sets. Extend S union I to a globally
maximal sum-free set. No further B-vertex can be added, so the final
intersection with B is I, and distinct I have distinct extensions.
Hence

                    f_max(F_5^k)>=3^(n/10-1/2).

These bounds prove (T). The paper proof is complete.

The cited external inputs, their exact formulations, and primary URLs
are recorded in global_final_bridge_audit.txt Section7. In particular,
the structure input is Lev's Theorem3 from JCTA205(2024),105865, and
the removal input is Green's Theorem1.5 with the three sets F,F,-F.
