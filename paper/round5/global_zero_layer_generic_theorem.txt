ALL SEEDS MEETING THE ZERO LAYER: THE GENERIC-COMPONENT TARGET
Status: assembled complete paper proof, with finite exact integer
certificates for the explicitly identified rank-one and rank-two models.
This is the zero-containing local part of the completed joint theorem in
global_final_coverage.txt and global_final_bridge_audit.txt. The no-zero
family is proved separately. No new Lean verification is claimed.

THEOREM.
Let G=F_5 x H, B={2,3}xH, and let S subset G\B be sum-free with
S intersect ({0}xH) nonempty. Apply a shear preserving B if useful and
let K be the span of all seed tails. Write kappa=|K|. The generic link
block on {2,3}x((g+K) union(-g+K)), for g not in K, satisfies
    mis(block)<=3^kappa.
Equivalently the exponent is one quarter of the block's 4kappa vertices.
There is no size or rank restriction on S in this generic statement.

Consequently the full link graph satisfies
    mis(L_S[B])<=3^{|H|/2+kappa/6}.
The kappa/6 term comes entirely from applying Moon--Moser to the finite
exceptional core. It is not silently omitted or asserted to be uniform
when kappa grows with |H|.

NOTATION.
A=S_0, P=S_1, Q=-S_4, z=|A|, p=|P|, q=|Q|,
a0=|A union -A|, d0=|P union Q|. The generic block has half its vertices
in each of the degree classes
    a0+z+d0+p, a0+z+d0+q.
All generic vertices are loop-free. Edges preserve a quotient coset or
reverse its sign, so distinct generic blocks and the core do not meet.

PROOF BY SEED SIZE.

1. Pure zero layer, any size.
The complete local full-link theorem is zero_pure_layer_theorem.txt.
It uses the general regular-graph potential for degree>=10; the few
smaller degrees are symmetric, single-defect, small-seed, triangle-free
nine-regular, or the separately proved zero-sum rank-two triple.
It has no rank bound and no exceptional-core error. Its componentwise
proof also yields the generic target. Alternatively use the amplification
observation at the end of this file.

2. |S|>=8, with at least one zero-layer seed.
Order p>=q. Put alpha=a0+z>=3, a=alpha+d0+p, b=alpha+d0+q.
Then a<=2b-3 and b>=a0+|S|>=10. Theorem C of
global_general_potential_audit.txt applies. Its fixed potential has
beta=c/(a+b-4), c=log_2(3)/3, and initial exponent exactly3c/4.
The proof uses a general hereditary maximum-degree master lemma;
it does not assume that induced subgraphs retain two degree classes.

3. |S|=6 or 7.
The exhaustive paper classification is global_small_seed_potential.txt.
If a0>=4 or the union P union Q is large enough, the smaller degree
is at least10 and theorem C applies. Otherwise A lies in one antipodal
direction. Nested layer sets, or A=-A, give triangle-free generic blocks.
The few remaining size-six patterns are the degree-cap lemmas12/9
with six low neighbors and10/9 with five low neighbors and two retained
neighborhood edges. Those lemmas explicitly keep original degree caps
and a fixed potential in every residual graph.
Independent audit: zero_small_seed_audit.txt.

4. |S|=5.
The exhaustive classification is global_sizefive_progress.txt.
The broad subcases follow from theorem C, the triangle-free degree
potentials, or the symmetric-core theorems. The remaining nine-regular
family is proved by a same-coordinate partner induction with six common
neighbors and two protected neighbors; its proof handles every induced
subgraph. The10/8 family is proved in global_ten_eight_proof.txt using
fixed degree caps, maximal neighbor deficit, and one extra branch in
the full graph. The8/8 triangle-free case uses zero_eight_extension.txt.
The triangle-bearing8/8 case has normalized form
    A={a}, P={0,r}, Q={0,r+a}.
Independent a,r give the explicit100-point transfer in
matrix_size5_triangle_proof.txt; dependent a,r leave only r=2a,
covered by global_rank1_certificate.txt.

5. |S|=4.
Every distribution and disposition is recorded in
global_mixed_size4_table.txt. Three normalized rank-two models require

    A={a},    P={0,r}, Q={0};
    A={a},    P={0,r}, Q={a};

For independent a,r all are proved in matrix_size4_proof.txt. The

6. |S|=3.
If all seeds lie in the zero layer, use step1. If exactly two do, then
dependent tails must be opposite and the seed is symmetric core plus
one; independent tails give the7/8 generic graph proved in
zero_eight_potential_audit.txt. If exactly one seed lies in the zero
layer, normalize one nonzero tail to zero by a shear. Independent
remaining directions give exactly the same-layer and opposite-layer
rank-two models in matrix_mixed3_strip_proof.txt. Their integer upper
bounds are112,742,891,520 and128,849,018,880, each below3^25.
Dependent directions give the five twenty-point triple certificates
in global_rank1_certificate.txt, apart from the already symmetric-core
case. Thus no affine-rank assumption is left implicit.

7. |S|<=2.
Use the complete non-enumerative paper proofs in
round3/paper_algebra_lowrank.txt, Sections 1--7. The generic counts or

All other one/two-seed cases have no zero element and are treated by the
no-zero theorem. The old round-two finite enumeration is not needed.

This exhausts every seed meeting the zero layer.

FULL-LINK CONSEQUENCE AND CORE ACCOUNTING.
Let h=|H|. The exceptional core has2kappa vertices, so its maximal
independent set count is at most3^{2kappa/3} after removing forbidden
loop vertices. There are(h/kappa-1)/2 generic blocks, each bounded by
3^kappa. Multiplication gives
    3^{2kappa/3} * (3^kappa)^{(h/kappa-1)/2}
       =3^{h/2+kappa/6}.

AMPLIFICATION OBSERVATION FOR EARLIER FULL-LINK THEOREMS.
Suppose an earlier theorem gives the target full-link upper bound for
a fixed seed embedded in every enlarged ambient H. In the extension
H=K direct-sum F_5^m, the same generic block occurs(5^m-1)/2 times and
the core has at least one maximal independent set. If its count is C,
then C^{(5^m-1)/2}<=3^{kappa5^m/2}. Letting m tend to infinity yields
C<=3^kappa. This justifies generic use of a full-link theorem even when
one prefers not to re-run its fiber-local proof on a single block.
