Notation: p=|P|>=q=|Q|>0, d=|P union Q|. A generic component has
4|K| vertices. Exactly half have degree d+p and half have degree d+q.
The target is mis<=3^{|K|}. This note concerns generic components;
the bounded-rank exceptional core is still handled separately.

1. AN OLD RESULT THAT MUST NOT BE LOST IN THE CLASSIFICATION

round2/algebraic_proofs.txt, Proposition 3, proves the full nested case
P subset Q or Q subset P, without a size or rank restriction. It is
stronger than just the symmetric-core-plus-one-defect case.

For Q subset P, the two left row neighborhoods at x satisfy
  N(L1_x)=(x+P) in R1 union (x+Q) in R2
      subset
  N(L2_x)=(x+P) in R1 union (x+P) in R2.
In a maximal independent set, choosing L2_x forces choosing L1_x;
otherwise L1_x could be added. Thus its left pair has only the three
states empty,{L1_x},{L1_x,L2_x}. The entire maximal independent set is
uniquely determined by its left half, giving at most 3^{|K|} choices.
The other inclusion reverses the two roles. This works even for
arbitrary Boolean matrices whose two rows at every index are nested.

4. THE THREE PATTERNS ISOLATED BY THE REDUCTION (NOW PROVED)

All three have exactly four distinct translations in P union Q:

 A: |P|=3, |Q|=1, P intersect Q=empty; degrees 7 and 5.
 B: |P|=2, |Q|=2, P intersect Q=empty; degrees 6 and 6.
 C: |P|=3, |Q|=2, |P intersect Q|=1; degrees 7 and 6.

A simultaneous translation P->P-t,Q->Q-t is just a column relabeling
of the generic biadjacency matrix. Normalize one union element to zero.
The remaining three translations span dimension at most three. Hence
these are finite affine configurations of rank at most three, not an
unbounded-rank family. The rank-one cases already follow from the
C4-factor paper estimate q<=5. In affine rank three, all four union
points are affinely independent, so each of A,B,C has one normal form
up to invertible linear maps, point relabeling and layer swap:

 A: P={e1,e2,e3}, Q={0};
 B: P={0,e1}, Q={e2,e3};
 C: P={0,e1,e2}, Q={0,e3}.

The resulting generic graphs have 500 vertices. Rank-two affine
relations among the four union points are not covered merely by a
rank-three representative. The final entropy arguments for A/C work
in every rank. For B, the collision classification and Boolean common
factor in matrix_typeB_entropy_proof.txt Section 7 remove that issue
without a rank-two representative list.

5. SCOPE CHECK FOR ALL THREE-ELEMENT SEEDS

The number of zero-layer seeds exhausts the possibilities:
 - three: all pure-zero layers are already proved;
 - two: if their tails are dependent, sum-freeness forces an opposite
   pair, hence the single-defect theorem. If independent, normalize
   the nonzero tail to zero and use zero_eight_potential_audit.txt;
 - one: normalize one nonzero tail to zero. Tail rank is at most two.
   Rank one uses the preceding finite classification. Rank two gives
   exactly the same/opposite models proved in matrix_mixed3_strip_proof;
 - zero: pure layers are proved; the only mixed case is a 2+1 split,
   normalized tail rank at most two, handled by (c).

Therefore every generic component for |S|<=3 obeys the target, with the
usual fixed-size exceptional-core allowance in the global asymptotics.
