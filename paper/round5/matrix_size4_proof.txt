ROUND 5: THREE MIXED SIZE-FOUR MODELS, WITH SHORT TRANSFER CERTIFICATES

Let a,p be linearly independent over F_5. We use normalized seed notation
  A=S_0, P=S_1, Q=-S_4.
This file proves the generic-component bound for the following models:

  nested:    A={a},    P={0,p}, Q={0};
  triangle:  A={a},    P={0,p}, Q={a};
  twozero:   A={a,-a}, P={0,p}, Q=empty.

All three generic graphs have 100 vertices. Their degree pairs, with
50 vertices of each degree, are respectively (7,6), (8,7), and (8,6).
The middle graph has triangles. No argument about adding ordinary edges
being monotone for maximal independent sets is used.

THEOREM.
For the three respective generic components,

  nested:   mis <=70*40^5  =  7,168,000,000;
  triangle: mis <=295*65^5 =342,285,734,375;
  twozero:  mis <=75*36^5  =  4,534,963,200.

Each quantity is strictly smaller than 3^25=847,288,609,443.

1. COMMON COORDINATES

For t outside K=span{a,p}, use rows
  a_x=(2,t+x), b_x=(3,t+x),
  c_x=(3,-t-x), d_x=(2,-t-x), x=x_0 a+x_1 p in K.
Number rows a,b,c,d by 0,1,2,3 and assign strip coordinates

  r=2x_1+1-(row mod 2) in Z_10,
  h=floor(row/2) in {0,1}, j=x_0 in Z_5.

Even-r slices contain b,d and odd-r slices contain a,c. Every slice has
W={0,1} x Z_5. There are no edges between nonadjacent distinct slices.

For nested and triangle, the internal graph F has a 5-cycle in each
h-row and the matching (1,j)--(0,j+1); it is C_5 square K_2.
For twozero, the internal graph F' joins (h,j) to (k,j+1) and (k,j-1)
for every h,k. Thus F' is the graph obtained by replacing each vertex
of C_5 by an independent pair and every cycle edge by a complete K_2,2.

Define three directed interfaces:
  J: (h,j)->(k,j), all h,k;
  L: (h,j)->(k,j), k<=h;
  D: (h,j)->(k,j), k<=h, and (h,j)->(k,j+1), h<=k.

The interfaces from even to odd, and from odd to the next even, are

       model       even->odd     odd->even
       nested          J             L
       triangle        D^T           L
       twozero         L^T           L.                     (1)

For example, the common translation zero in P,Q creates the complete
relation J. If Q={a}, the P0 and Qa edges form D from the a,c slice to
the b,d slice, so their orientation from even to odd is D^T. The Pp
edges always give L to the following even slice. With Q empty, P0 alone
gives L^T in the other direction. The one or two zero seeds give F or
F', respectively. These statements also follow by direct substitution
in the link condition y+z,y-z,z-y in S.

The verifier independently constructs each graph in F_5^4 from its
actual seed (remembering Q=-S_4), checks seed sum-freeness and absence
of generic loops, and compares all 100 adjacency lists with (1).

2. A PRECISE FINITE TRANSFER

Let I be all independent subsets of the internal graph (81 for F,
61 for F'). Write D_0(S)=W\(S union N(S)). For an interface C let
Out_C(L) be its neighbors reached from L and In_C(T) the vertices
that have an interface neighbor in T.

A phase-i state is (S,U), where S belongs to I and for some L in I,
  Out_Cprev(L) intersect S=empty,
  U=D_0(S)\Out_Cprev(L).
Keep only states for which some T in I satisfies
  Out_Ci(S) intersect T=empty, U subset In_Ci(T).

From a retained state (S,U) to a retained next-phase state (T,V), a
transition exists exactly when
  Out_Ci(S) intersect T=empty,
  U subset In_Ci(T),
  V=D_0(T)\Out_Ci(S).                                       (2)

A maximal independent set gives its selected slice sets S_r. Then
U_r consists exactly of vertices not selected or internally dominated,
and not dominated from slice r-1. Maximality is precisely the second
condition in (2), domination from slice r+1. Conversely a closed walk
obeying (2) gives an independent set that dominates every omitted
vertex. The U_r are determined by consecutive selected slice sets,
so this is a bijection with length-ten closed walks.

The retained states and one-step transition counts are

       model       states phase0/phase1     edges phase0/phase1
       nested              100/70                  615/340
       triangle            295/340                 925/3875
       twozero              75/75                  390/390.

Let T_0,T_1 denote the two transition matrices. For nested choose phase
one and B=T_1 T_0 (70 states). For the other two choose phase zero and
B=T_0 T_1 (295 and 75 states). The bijection gives

                            mis=tr(B^5).                     (3)

3. THE INTEGER CERTIFICATES

Set w=B1, the row-sum vector of the two-step matrix. The complete
finite certificate is

       w>0 and Bw<=lambda*w,

with lambda=40,65,36 for nested, triangle, twozero. All entries are
integers. In fact the largest ratios (Bw)_i/w_i are

       nested:   3481/88 <40;
       triangle: 1601/25 <65;
       twozero:  457/13  <36.

The tables below list every rotation orbit in the selected phase,
with columns S,U,w,Bw. Encode (h,j) by bit 5h+j. Rotation j->j+1 acts
simultaneously on S and U and has orbit size five on every retained
state in the selected phase. Thus the three tables contain 14,59,15
rows, respectively. Every state and transition is defined explicitly
by (1),(2); the certificate involves no numerical eigenvalue.

For reference, the interface row masks are
  J=(33,66,132,264,528,33,66,132,264,528),
  L=(1,2,4,8,16,33,66,132,264,528),
  D=(67,134,268,536,49,97,194,388,776,560).

The standard-library verifier constructs all states and transitions,
forms B exactly, checks all table orbits and checks every integer
inequality. A positive vector satisfying Bw<=lambda*w gives
B^5w<=lambda^5*w. Therefore (B^5)_{ii}<=lambda^5, and (3) implies

                        mis<=dim(B)*lambda^5.

This proves all three asserted upper bounds.

As an optional stronger calculation using the same exact transfer,
the maximal-independent-set counts are
  nested:    74,668,930;
  triangle: 770,498,435;
  twozero:   30,758,595.
These exact counts are not needed for the certificates and are not
claimed to come from an independent counting method.

4. SCOPE AND FILES

Every opposite pair of nonzero quotient cosets of H/K gives the same
100-vertex generic graph. The finite exceptional core over K is treated
by the existing global reduction. No estimate for that core is silently
inferred from this generic theorem.

The fourth model A={a,-a},P={0},Q={p} is already a symmetric zero-layer
core plus two defects in opposite nonzero layers, so the round-four
two-opposite-defects theorem covers it without a new certificate.
