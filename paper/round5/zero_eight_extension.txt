ROUND 5: REMOVING THE LOW-NEIGHBOR CONDITION AT MAXIMUM DEGREE EIGHT
Status: complete paper proof with exact integer certificates.
The extension uses the proved maximum-degree-seven portion of
zero_eight_potential_audit.txt. No Lean verification is claimed.

THEOREM.
For every finite simple triangle-free graph F of maximum degree at most
eight,
        mis(F) <= 2^{mu(F)},
        mu(F)=(16|V(F)|-|E(F)|)/31.                          (1)

PROOF.
Induct on the number of vertices, using additivity of mu and
multiplicativity of mis to reduce to a connected graph. Graphs of
maximum degree at most seven satisfy (1) by Sections 2--4 of
zero_eight_potential_audit.txt; no low-neighbor assumption occurs there.
Thus the current maximum degree is eight.

First suppose the graph is not eight-regular. A path from a degree-eight
vertex to a smaller-degree vertex contains an edge vw with deg(v)=8 and
deg(w)<=7. Deleting v drops the potential by 8/31. Since the graph is
triangle-free, at most 7*8+7=63 edges meet N[v], and deleting N[v] drops
the potential by at least (16*9-63)/31=81/31. The branch coefficient is
strictly smaller than
        46/55+9/55=1,
by the exact comparisons 2^(-8/31)<46/55 and 2^(-81/31)<9/55.

It remains to handle an eight-regular connected graph. Fix a vertex u
and four distinct neighbors v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3 of u. Triangle-freeness makes
these four vertices pairwise nonadjacent. Define
        F_j=F-{v_0,...,v_{j-1}},       j=0,1,2,3,4.
At stage j<=3 the vertex v_j still has degree eight, while u has degree
8-j. All other neighbors of v_j have degree at most eight. Therefore
  mu(F_j)-mu(F_{j+1}) = 8/31,
  mu(F_j)-mu(F_j-N_{F_j}[v_j]) >= (80+j)/31.
The second assertion uses the exact triangle-free closed-neighborhood
edge count: at most (8-j)+7*8=64-j edges are removed.

Expand include/exclude branching successively at v_0,...,v_3, continuing
only the exclude branch. Every resulting graph is a proper induced
subgraph, so the inductive hypothesis gives

  mis(F)/2^{mu(F)}
      <= 2^(-32/31)+sum_{j=0}^3 2^{-(80+9j)/31}.

The five terms, in the order with numerators 32,80,89,98,107, are
strictly smaller than
        489/1000, 21/125, 137/1000, 14/125, 23/250,
respectively. These rational bounds sum to 499/500<1. This closes the
eight-regular case and proves (1).

TWO-DEGREE COROLLARY.
If F is triangle-free, half its vertices have degree a and half degree
b, where max(a,b)<=8 and a+b>=15, then
        mu(F)/|V(F)| <= 49/124 < log_2(3)/4.
The strict comparison is exactly 2^49<3^31. Consequently
        mis(F)<3^{|V(F)|/4}
for every nonempty such graph. This includes all eight-regular graphs
and the previously proved 8/7 generic component.

CERTIFICATE.
Every inequality 2^(-a/31)<p/q is certified by the integer comparison
        q^31 < p^31*2^a.
The seven required comparisons, the rational sum 499/500, and the final
target comparison are checked in zero_verify_eight_extension.py.
